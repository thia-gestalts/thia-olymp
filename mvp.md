# First usable Olymp

A learner creates an account with a login name and password, signs in from a second device, and receives the entire base. Normal registration and sign-in need no email service. Reuse client-side unlocking through the OPAQUE flow. A small web page shows connected apps/devices and sync status where known. Show a record count only if it is available without exposing plaintext to the service.

Clients commit offline and sync automatically on connection, app launch and while active. A retry preserves event identity. An offline phone can add an image note while a browser records practice. Both arrive intact in desktop and in each other’s local replica. Mark a client fully synced only after referenced attachments have arrived. Surface quota and connectivity failures.

MVP recovery covers signing in on a replacement device with the learner’s credentials or repopulating from a surviving complete replica. Account email, forgotten-password recovery keys, deletion history and trash are deferred.
